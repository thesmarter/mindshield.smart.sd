Privacy Policy

This policy covers the MindShield Android app and this website. It is written in plain language, and it describes what actually happens rather than what sounds good.

The short version

Your attention score, blocking rules, goals, and reminders stay on your device. Installed apps and per-app usage stay on the device too, unless you accept the one-time Data sharing disclosure that uploads them to your own private account so recommendations and app classifications stay accurate; declining keeps everything local. An account is optional and the app works offline. There is no advertising and no advertising identifier. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash diagnostics and Firebase Analytics collects aggregated feature counts, never your app list or per-app usage.

Who we are and what this policy covers

MindShield is an Android screen-time and focus app published by the MindShield team. This policy covers the app and its marketing website. It does not cover the app stores themselves; each store has its own privacy policy.

For privacy or data questions, email info@smart.sd.

What stays on your device

The following stays on your device by default and does not leave it unless you explicitly choose a feature that requires it:

  • Installed applications, their categories, and per-app usage.
  • Block rules, schedules, daily limits, and temporary bypasses.
  • Goals, tasks, habits, their logs, and milestones.
  • Focus sessions and progress statistics.
  • App settings, appearance, language, and reminders.

Package names and per-app usage are never sent to analytics or crash reporting. After you accept App-data sharing, they are written to your own rows on Supabase so your recommendations follow you between sign-ins; while sharing is off they never leave the device and disappear when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What leaves the device, with your permission

  • Learning plans: only the goal text, level, language, weekly minutes, and preferred formats are sent to a Supabase Edge Function. If an AI provider is configured, the text is processed there only to generate the plan, without your app list or usage history.
  • Optional sync: uploads goals, milestones, tasks, habits, habit logs, and focus summaries to Supabase. It is off by default.
  • App-data sharing: after the disclosure is accepted, the installed-app list plus seven days of per-app minutes and launch counts are uploaded to your own Supabase rows. You can see and stop this at any time in Settings. It only runs while the server confirms the feature is enabled.
  • Notifications: a Firebase Cloud Messaging token is stored while signed in so the admin team can send service notices, and it is deleted with the account.
  • Diagnostics: Crashlytics receives crash stack traces and Analytics receives aggregated feature counts. Neither receives package names, app labels, or per-app durations.
  • Account: sign-in and account deletion use Supabase Auth.

Providers acting on our behalf We use Supabase for the database, authentication, and functions; Firebase for analytics, crash reporting, and notifications; and an AI provider when one is configured for learning plans. These services act as technical providers. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

What is never collected

We do not collect an advertising identifier, a device fingerprint, an install ID, location, contacts, messages, photos, files, calendar, clipboard, screen content, keystrokes, or passwords.

The optional accessibility service reads only the package identifier of the app that came to the foreground and cannot retrieve window content. Its configuration declares canRetrieveWindowContent="false".

Your app list is never sent to analytics, crash reports, or AI requests.

The website

This website is static. It uses no tracking cookies, advertising, or third-party tracking tags, and serves fonts and images locally. To improve it we count page views and download-link clicks (path, locale, referrer source and host, UTM, device class, and country from the hosting provider's header) without any visitor identifier, IP, or raw User-Agent; measurement can be disabled by the operations team at any time.

To load the current store links, your browser contacts a Supabase endpoint. Supabase receives the usual technical request data, such as your IP address and user agent, only to serve the request. We do not use that visit to build a profile or link it to your account.

The email button opens your device's mail app. There is no contact form collecting data on the site.

Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Every database table enforces row level security, so you can only read and write your own rows. The app ships only the publishable API key; service keys never leave the server.

No system is completely secure. If you notice a vulnerability or unexpected behaviour, email dev@smart.sd.

Your choices

  • Revoke usage access, overlay access, accessibility, or notifications at any time in Android Settings; the app degrades instead of failing.
  • Turn app-data sharing on or off at any time in Privacy and access, Data sharing.
  • Use the app fully offline with no account.
  • Export or delete your local data from Privacy and access.
  • Delete your account from the Account screen in the app, or from the account deletion page.

Delete your account on the web: Account deletion page

Retention and deletion

Deleting your account immediately removes your profile, goals, tasks, habits, focus summaries, and device token. App-inventory rows created by an accepted disclosure are unlinked from your account and kept only as anonymous aggregate statistics. Local data stays on your device until you delete it in the app, clear its data, or uninstall it.

A technical provider may retain backups or technical logs for the period required by its systems and policies, without using them for an advertising profile.

Children

MindShield is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child created an account, email us so we can review and delete it.

Changes to this policy

When app behaviour or the scope of data changes, we update this policy and its last-updated date. If the change is material, we will explain it in the app or on the site before it takes effect.
