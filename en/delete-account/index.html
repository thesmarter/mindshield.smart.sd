Delete your account

You can delete your MindShield account at any time from inside the app. This page explains what is deleted, what remains, and how to get help if you cannot sign in.

Last updated: 23 November 2026 Home info@smart.sd

On this page

  1. Delete from the app
  2. What is deleted
  3. What remains
  4. If you cannot sign in

Delete from the app

  • Open MindShield and go to Account.
  • Choose Delete account.
  • Confirm when the warning appears.

Deletion is immediate; there is no waiting period. You are not asked for an extra password if you are already signed in, but the system may ask for confirmation to prevent accidental deletion.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and account data.
  • Your goals, tasks, habits, and their logs.
  • Goal milestones and focus-session summaries.
  • Your Firebase Cloud Messaging device token.

What remains

App-inventory rows created by an accepted data-sharing disclosure are unlinked from your account and kept only as anonymous aggregate statistics. They are no longer associated with you.

Local data on your device, such as goals, tasks, habits, focus sessions, and settings, remains until you delete it in the app, clear its data, or uninstall it. You can export or delete it from Privacy and access before deleting your account.

If you cannot sign in

Email us from the address linked to the account if possible, and say that you want to delete it. We may ask for proof of ownership before acting, to protect your data.

Email: info@smart.sd

A note Do not send your password or verification code by email. We will guide you to the next safe step.
